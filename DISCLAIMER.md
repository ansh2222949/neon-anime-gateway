### 1. Zero Hosting Policy
* **Neon Anime Gateway does NOT host, archive, store, or distribute any video or audio files on its own servers or cloud storage.**
* All anime metadata, cover art, and descriptions are dynamically retrieved via official public GraphQL APIs provided by [AniList](https://anilist.co).
